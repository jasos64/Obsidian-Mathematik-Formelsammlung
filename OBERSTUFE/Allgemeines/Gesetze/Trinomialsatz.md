> Sei $\left(x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n\right)^3$, dann gilt:

$$
\begin{align}
	\left(x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n\right)^3&=
	\sum_{k=1}^{n}x_{k}^{3}+3x_{k}^{2}\cdot
	\left(\sum_{i=k+1}^{n}x_{i}\right)+3x_{k}\cdot
	\left(\sum_{j=k+1}^{n}x_{j}^{2}\right)+6\cdot e_{3}\left(x_{1},x_{2},...,x_{n}\right)
\end{align}
$$

$$
\left(x_{1}+x_{2}+\cdots+x_{n}\right)^{2}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(x_{k}^{2}+2x_{k}\sum_{i=k+1}^{n}x_{i}\right)
$$
